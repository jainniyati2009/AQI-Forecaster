How is AQI Predicted?

Stage 1: WRF-Chem

High-resolution coupled meteorological-chemical transport model. Simulates atmospheric physics (wind, temp, PBL height) interlinked with chemical species (PM2.5, PM10, O₃, NOx). Captures two-way feedback between weather and pollutants.

Stage 2: TensorFlow Model 2

ML regression model trained on previous years' data. Adjusts WRF-Chem baseline for:

  • Time of year (crop burning season + traffic patterns)
  • Day of week (weekday vs weekend emissions)
  • National holidays (Republic Day, Diwali fireworks, etc.)

Key Innovation

Unlike standard models, AirAware captures the dynamic feedback loop: atmospheric inversion traps PM2.5 → PM2.5 blocks sunlight → reduced temperature → weaker mixing → lower PBL → more trapping. This coupled approach yields significantly more accurate 72-hour forecasts.
